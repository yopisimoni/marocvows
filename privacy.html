Privacy summary

MarocVows separates ordinary public-directory browsing from account, wedding-help, provider-application and community features. The public directory can be used without creating an account.

When a user voluntarily submits information through an enabled workflow, MarocVows should use only the information needed for that purpose and protect it according to the applicable privacy process. Users should avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents.

Corrections and requests

Privacy questions and personal-data requests can be sent through the contact details published by MarocVows. Provider-directory corrections should identify the listing and the information that needs review.

Third-party provider websites, social profiles and contact channels have their own privacy practices. Leaving MarocVows to contact a provider means the provider’s own terms and privacy practices may apply.
