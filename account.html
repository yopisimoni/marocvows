Account access and privacy

MarocVows uses passwordless account access where enabled. The public wedding directory does not require an account, and account-only tools remain separate from normal browsing.

Never send passwords or payment-card information through account support. If an authentication option is not shown as active, it should not be treated as available. Wedding-help and provider workflows may remain unavailable until their production and privacy checks are complete.

Accounts are intended to help users manage supported requests, not to replace direct confirmation with wedding providers. Prices, availability, contracts and deposits must still be confirmed with the relevant professional.
