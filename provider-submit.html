Apply to be listed on MarocVows.

If you provide a real wedding service in Morocco, you can send your business details for review. Submitting does not automatically publish a listing; MarocVows checks the information first.

Who can apply?

Caterers, wedding venues, wedding planners, cooks and kitchen teams, DJs, photographers, videographers, decorators, bands and musicians, florists, beauty professionals, henna artists, wedding transport providers, cake and pastry businesses, event-rental companies, and other legitimate wedding services.

Use a MarocVows account

Provider applications require an account so the applicant can later see the review status and correct information if needed.

How listings are treated

MarocVows does not manufacture star ratings. New providers begin without a community score. If community reviews are enabled later, only approved MarocVows reviews can affect the displayed rating.
