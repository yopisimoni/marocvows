What to include in your message

For a provider correction, include the provider name, city, the detail that needs changing and a reliable public source when one is available. That helps MarocVows review the request without guessing.

For wedding-help questions, share your city, approximate date, guest count and the services you are trying to find. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents.

Wedding professionals asking about a listing should include the business name, service category, city, current contact information and an official website or social profile when available. Submissions are reviewed before publication and do not guarantee ranking, verification or placement.

Response scope

MarocVows can correct directory information and help explain how to use the service, but bookings, prices, availability and contracts are handled directly between couples and providers.
