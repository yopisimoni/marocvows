Community principles

MarocVows community contributions must be based on genuine experiences and must not contain invented ratings, impersonation, spam or misleading promotional claims. Reviews should describe the contributor’s own experience and distinguish facts from personal opinion.

Photos and reports should be relevant to the provider or event being discussed. Contributors should not upload private documents, passwords, payment details or images they do not have permission to share.

Provider fairness

Providers may request factual corrections through the contact channel. A correction request does not guarantee removal of a genuine review, and paid relationships must not be presented as independent recommendations.

Community features may remain disabled until moderation and abuse controls are ready. These guidelines explain the standard that will apply when those features are available.
