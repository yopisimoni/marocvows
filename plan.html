How to use your checklist

Start with the services that affect the rest of the event plan: venue, caterer and planner or coordinator where needed. Then add photography, music, decoration, beauty, transport and other services according to the type of celebration you are organizing.

The checklist is a planning aid, not a booking engine. After opening a provider category, compare the information available for your city and contact professionals directly. Confirm your date, venue, guest count, service area and any access or travel requirements before asking for a final quote.

What to confirm before paying a deposit

Ask for the exact deliverables, total price, deposit schedule, extra charges, setup and service times, cancellation terms and the name of the person or team responsible on the event day. Written confirmation makes different offers easier to compare.

Provider availability and public listing details can change. MarocVows does not guarantee that a listed professional is available for a specific date, so final confirmation always comes from the provider.
