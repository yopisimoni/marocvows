Wedding DJ in Morocco: prices, equipment and questions to ask

“Wedding DJ” can mean a simple music service or a full setup with sound, lighting, microphones, hosting and event coordination. Here is how to compare packages properly.

Published prices do not always cover the same service. Party.ma currently shows roughly 1,200–6,000 MAD per evening in Casablanca and Rabat, including about 2,000–3,500 MAD for a standard wedding and 3,500–6,000 MAD for a premium wedding. Wervice publishes a broader “music / DJ / band” category from 3,000–60,000 MAD depending on tier. Those figures should not be treated as directly equivalent.

Best question: do not ask only “How much is the DJ?” Ask what is included? Sound system, lighting, microphones, setup, performance hours, hosting, travel and overtime can all change the final price.

What a DJ package may include

  • DJ performance and music preparation before the event.
  • Sound system sized for the venue and guest count.
  • Mixer, speakers and monitoring.
  • Microphones for announcements or ceremony moments.
  • Dance-floor or ambient lighting.
  • Setup, sound check and teardown.
  • Microphone hosting when included.
  • Moroccan, international or mixed music styles.

10 questions before booking

  1. How many hours are included?
  2. What sound equipment is included?
  3. Is lighting included?
  4. How many microphones are provided?
  5. Is there backup equipment?
  6. Can we prepare must-play and do-not-play lists?
  7. Can the DJ handle different generations and music styles?
  8. Who will actually perform on the wedding day?
  9. Which travel or overtime fees can be added?
  10. What deposit, cancellation and date-change terms apply?

Moroccan weddings: plan the transitions

A Moroccan wedding can move between entrances, dinner, family moments, traditional music, dance and international styles. Discuss the timeline before the event so the DJ knows when music should support an entrance, when volume should drop and how to move smoothly between different generations and tastes.
