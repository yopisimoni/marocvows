Wedding budget · Morocco · 2026

How much does a wedding in Morocco cost in 2026?

There is no single “Moroccan wedding price.” This guide explains the published ranges, why they differ so much, and how to build a budget you can later replace with real quotes.

The key point: public 2026 prices do not all describe the same market. Local platforms may show accessible packages in Fès or Meknès, while national or premium guides publish far higher figures. That difference often reflects city, guest count, service level and—most importantly—what is included.

Quick rule: start with guest count, venue type and service level. Then replace every published range with a dated quote for your city and wedding date.

Why the spread matters: a 10,000 MAD venue that charges separately for furniture, security, sound and cleaning can end up costing more than an 18,000 MAD venue with those elements included. The same applies to per-person catering prices.

The MarocVows budget formula

Starting budget =
Venue + (catering per person × guests) + Negafa + Photo/Video + Music + Décor + Outfits + Cake + optional Planner + safety margin.

This is not a market price. It is a planning framework to help you avoid forgetting major categories and to show what changes when the guest list grows.

Three ways to think about the budget

Essential

Simple venue or lower-demand date, classic menu, lighter Negafa package, DJ rather than orchestra and controlled décor.

Comfort

More sought-after venue, complete catering, photo + video, fuller Negafa service and more elaborate décor.

Prestige

Exceptional venue, larger guest list, premium food, high-end staging, bigger teams and well-known providers.

Seven questions before paying a deposit

  1. What exactly is included?
  2. Does the final guest count change the price?
  3. Which fees are separate?
  4. How much is the deposit and when is the balance due?
  5. What happens if the date changes?
  6. Who will actually deliver the service on the day?
  7. Is this quote valid for your city and date?
