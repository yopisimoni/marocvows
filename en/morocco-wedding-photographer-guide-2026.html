Wedding photographer in Morocco: prices, packages and questions to ask

Choosing a photographer is not only about the best images in a portfolio. You also need to compare coverage hours, deliverables, editing, video, albums, travel and image-use terms.

Published ranges depend on scope. Party.ma currently publishes reference prices in Rabat, Casablanca and Marrakech of about 2,500–4,000 MAD for a half-day wedding, 4,000–8,000 MAD for a full wedding day and 6,000–15,000 MAD for photo + video. Wervice publishes a broader “photography + film” category ranging roughly from 8,000 to 70,000 MAD by tier. Those figures may represent different hours, team sizes and deliverables.

Best practice: compare photographers using the same brief: date, locations, hours, photography-only or photo + video, team size, album requirements, travel and delivery deadline.

What a photography package may include

  • Bride and/or groom preparations.
  • Entrances, ceremony, couple portraits and family photographs.
  • Reception coverage and key moments.
  • One or more photographers depending on the package.
  • Editing and digital delivery of selected images.
  • Album, prints or private gallery where included.
  • Video, highlight film or a video team when part of the package.
  • Travel between multiple locations or cities under the agreed terms.

Portfolio: ask to see a full wedding

Instagram and portfolio pages usually show the strongest images. Ask to see a complete wedding gallery or representative full report instead: preparation, low light, movement, group photos, details, reception and late-evening coverage. This helps you assess consistency rather than a handful of highlights.

10 questions before booking

  1. How many hours are included?
  2. Who will actually photograph the wedding?
  3. Is a second photographer or videographer included?
  4. How many edited images or what type of gallery will you receive?
  5. What is the delivery timeline?
  6. Are albums or prints included?
  7. Which travel fees may be added?
  8. How is overtime charged?
  9. What backup plan applies if the lead photographer cannot attend?
  10. What deposit, postponement and cancellation terms apply?

For couples planning from abroad

If you are organizing from France, Belgium, Spain, the UK or elsewhere, arrange a video call before paying a deposit and keep a written timeline. Share all addresses, identify your local contacts in Morocco and list the family moments you do not want missed. Keep any changes to hours or deliverables in writing.
